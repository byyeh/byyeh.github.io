Ankle Perturberator Robot Ongoing research

Neuromuscular and Rehabilitation Robotics Laboratory, University of Michigan, 2026 to present.
Mechanism design, contact and fatigue analysis, material selection.

Ankle impedance testing currently relies on an expensive lab robot that clinics can't access. I'm designing the latch for a low-cost alternative. It has to hold the platform against a 60 N·m torque, which puts 354 N on the latch, release on command from a solenoid, and reset with no power.

  • Designed a self-relocking 7075-T6 latch hook in SolidWorks, placed about the platform pivot to minimize latch force
  • Ran hook stress and hook-to-shaft contact analysis in ANSYS, with frictionless contact and a refined mesh at the contact face
  • Chose a contactless LVIT over encoder and IMU options for motion sensing
Results so far
Latch load analyzed354 N, from 60 N·m platform torque
Hook, 7075-T639.4 MPa max. Yield SF 12.8, fatigue SF 4.1
Shaft, 1215 steel73.5 MPa max. Yield SF 5.6, fatigue SF 3.7
Max deformation0.016 mm

MEMS microphone stack design

Logitech, 2025.
Vibration isolation, acoustic sealing, experimental testing.

Microphone audio on a video conference bar was picking up speaker vibration and leaking sound. I redesigned the microphone stack-up to improve vibration isolation and acoustic sealing together, instead of trading one for the other.

  • Designed four stack-ups in Creo, with tolerance analysis
  • Built a mockup of each one
  • Tested them on a custom DAQ and audio rig
Results
Vibration isolation+15 to 61%
Acoustic sealing+22 to 41%
Outcome2 product-tier options, submitted as a Logitech invention disclosure

Speaker suspension design

Logitech, 2025.
Suspension design, modal analysis, design of experiments.

In a tabletop video conferencing system, speaker vibration was leaking into the housing, and the bulky suspension was crowding the acoustic chamber. The goal was to isolate the speaker, free up chamber volume, and keep assembly simple.

  • Designed compact rubber mounts in Creo
  • Added mounting ears for simple assembly
  • Tuned the design with ANSYS modal analysis
Results
Vibration isolation+32%
Suspension size−66%
First mode28 Hz

Laptop hinge bracket optimization

Wistron, 2024.
Sheet metal design, stress analysis, design for manufacturing.

A laptop screen took 4.2 s to settle after an impact, with peak bracket stress at 1121 MPa. I had to shorten the settling time with no change to the chassis, using stamping only.

  • Iterated the stamped sheet metal geometry in Creo
  • Compared stress between designs in ANSYS
  • Validated prototypes with bouncing tests, using infrared laser sensors, force gauges and DAQ
Results
Settling time4.2 s → 2.5 s −40%
ProcessStamping, a lower-cost alternative to MIM

Organ-on-a-chip supply system

Micro-Systems and Control Laboratory, National Tsing Hua University, 2023 to 2024.
System architecture, spring mechanism design, user workflow design.

Organ-on-a-chip experiments rely on costly electric syringe pumps, which keeps them inside the lab. I designed a compact, electricity-free instrument that replaces the usual pump, tubing and collector setup.

  • Defined the system architecture and mapped the user workflow

  • Modeled the full instrument in Inventor
